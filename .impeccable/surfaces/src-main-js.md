# Surface Brief: ShopBoard Main Storefront

- **Mode:** Persuade (with integrated Operate controls for filtering, cart, coupon, and checkout)
- **Approved Comp:** `.impeccable/mocks/comp-1.png`
- **Audience & Job:** Engineers, developers, and designers assembling a precision desk setup in SAR, comparing technical specs and ordering directly.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** ShopBoard frames high-precision desk hardware inside an illuminated Akari paper-and-bamboo workshop ledger, refusing both sterile white tech-store grids and neon gamer dark-mode clichés.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Candlelit Washi Ivory ground (`#F6EFE1`), Paper Ash panels (`#E7E1D6`), crisp 1px Bamboo Tan structural ribs (`#D8B48A`), Rib Shadow dividers (`#C8BBA1`), deep Charcoal Ink typography and primary pill actions (`#2A2A2A`), and a square Vermilion seal stamp (`#CC3A2E`) with an Enso ink brush ring.
- **STORY:** The visitor experiences a calm, warm-lit craft studio of pro workspace equipment, trusts the engineering specs and 2-year warranty, and adds gear to their desk configuration.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Right-hand vertical bamboo-ribbed atelier sidebar (brand lockup with Vermilion seal stamp, vertical navigation links, Enso ink circle, and featured brass desk accessory card); upper hero stage on the left with a warm-lit oak desk setup photograph blending seamlessly into Washi Ivory beside the Charcoal display headline "ارتقِ بمساحة عملك", primary Charcoal pill CTA "استكشف المجموعة ←", secondary bamboo-bordered CTA "تكوين المكتب", and 3 bamboo-divided feature badges; lower 4-column bordered Washi hardware catalog grid below the horizontal rib divider.
